Delete Your Subul Account

Subul (سبل) allows users to request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to delete your account

You can delete your account directly from the Subul application:

  1. Open Subul.
  2. Open your Profile.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion.

Request deletion by email

You can also request account deletion by contacting:

ahmedhamdyabdallah93@gmail.com

Subject: Delete My Subul Account

Data that will be deleted

  • Account information
  • Profile information
  • Profile picture
  • Quran reading progress
  • Bookmarks
  • Prayer progress and logs
  • Azkar progress
  • User goals and associated user data

Data that may be retained

Some limited technical or security-related information may be retained when required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or operational purposes.
